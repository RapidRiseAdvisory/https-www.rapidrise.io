Commercial Architecture Review

A focused two week engagement that shows where your commercial architecture is working and where it is losing ground. It is delivered as a written report that stands on its own, with no ongoing commitment.

The review covers:

  • Deal pipeline assessment: where deals are stalling and why
  • Buyer mapping: whether you are reaching budget holders or influencers
  • Qualification gap analysis: where your process loses commercial ground
  • Messaging and positioning review: how your narrative lands with the economic buyer and with procurement
  • 90 day commercial roadmap: prioritised actions with clear ownership

The review is designed as the first step of any engagement. You see the quality of the thinking before committing to anything longer.
